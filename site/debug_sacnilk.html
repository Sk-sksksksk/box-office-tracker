Indian films released in 2026 have grossed ₹13,722.46 Cr worldwide so far: ₹10,932.65 Cr in India and ₹2,789.81 Cr overseas, so overseas markets make up 20.3% of the total. Counting every film released in India this year, including Hollywood and other foreign releases, the India box office stands at ₹12,492.10 Cr gross (₹10,666.64 Cr net), with foreign films contributing ₹1,559.45 Cr of that gross.
